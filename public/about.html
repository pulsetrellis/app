About PulseTrellis

Vital signs viewer with measurement context

Explore recorded vital measurements with original names, units, dates and source details. PulseTrellis makes the available context easy to review.

What this site does

Explore source-provided vital measurements with original names, units, dates, and source context.

A focused product

PulseTrellis focuses on vital signs viewer with measurement context. Its own interface, public documentation and source repository support the product’s continued development.

PulseTrellis uses FinchNode for the authorized vital-sign and encounter categories. Its own display preserves each observation’s supplied name and unit, while keeping encounter context separate from any unproven relationship to a measurement.

Current availability

Live record connections are awaiting production activation. Public guides are available now; deployment alone does not establish a completed patient connection.

Public information and private records

Guide search runs in your browser and covers public explanatory pages only. It does not access a patient session, index health records or send search terms to an analytics service. Opening the homepage or an article never starts a record import.

Read the privacy and data-handling notice before connecting. Medical questions belong with your care team; this site documents software behavior.

Source code and corrections

Inspect the public PulseTrellis repository for implementation, license, setup and tests. For privacy questions or factual corrections, contact software@council.health. Please do not include private health records in a public GitHub issue.
