Reading a vital-sign record: value, unit, date and source

Use PulseTrellis to inspect a recorded vital measurement while keeping its unit, date and source context attached to the value.

By the PulseTrellis project · Published September 9, 2026 · Software guide

On this page

  1. Begin with the measurement name
  2. Keep four details together
  3. Use the detail view to resolve display questions
  4. Avoid reading a trend into isolated entries
  5. Questions about this guide

Begin with the measurement name

Choose an observation from the list and read its source-provided label. PulseTrellis does not assume a familiar measurement type just because a value looks recognizable. The selected panel and detail view are the places to check what the entry actually contains.

Keep four details together

A number copied without its surrounding fields can lose the meaning the record supplied. When preparing a question about a measurement, keep its name, quantity and unit, date and source together. If the source did not provide a reference range, the app says so.

  1. Select one observation at a time.
  2. Read the displayed unit as part of the value.
  3. Check the available date before comparing entries.
  4. Expand “Show measurement details” to inspect the returned fields.

Use the detail view to resolve display questions

The JSON panel shows the normalized observation behind the visible measurement. It can help you identify a missing field or see why a label was chosen. It does not establish a diagnosis or supply measurements that were never returned.

Avoid reading a trend into isolated entries

PulseTrellis currently presents individual measurements. It does not infer target ranges, blood pressure categories or clinical trends. Discuss clinical meaning with an appropriate professional and refer to the original source when needed. Authorized imports remain pending production activation.

Questions about this guide

Does PulseTrellis classify a measurement as healthy or unhealthy?

No. The implemented display preserves source information without calculating a clinical classification.

Can I manually add a new measurement?

The current workspace inspects imported observations. It does not include a manual measurement-entry form.
